# ADR-003: Open-core Business Model

## Status

Proposed.

## Decision

Open-source the trust-critical local layer and monetize managed intelligence, synchronization, teams, and enterprise controls.

## Rationale

Developers can inspect collection behavior, contribute adapters, and adopt without procurement friction.

## Risk

A competitor could fork the client. Defensibility must come from data quality, longitudinal intelligence, ecosystem, and execution.
