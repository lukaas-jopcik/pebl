# Validation Plan

## Critical hypothesis 1

Users want feedback after AI work.

Test: concierge receipts for 100 real interactions.

Pass: at least 40% of receipts are rated useful and users apply at least 20% of suggestions.

## Critical hypothesis 2

Feedback can be specific without uploading source code.

Test: compare metadata-only, diff-summary, and full-context analysis.

Pass: metadata or local semantic summaries produce useful feedback for a meaningful share of tasks.

## Critical hypothesis 3

Pebl does not interrupt flow.

Test: automatic silent mode versus pre-coach suggestions.

Pass: less than 10% of users disable post-task receipts due to interruption.

## Critical hypothesis 4

Improvements carry forward.

Test: measure similar tasks before and after applying memory or prompt suggestions.

Pass: observable reduction in retries, rediscovery, or verification failures.
