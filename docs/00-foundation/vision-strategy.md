# Vision and Strategy

## Vision

Become the Human × AI Performance Layer across every AI workspace.

## Five-year outcome

Pebl becomes the neutral system of record for how individuals and teams collaborate with AI. It works across models, agents, repositories, and enterprise environments.

## Strategic wedge

Start with terminal-first AI-native developers because they:

- use agents repeatedly every day;
- understand open-source tooling;
- have measurable workflows;
- influence organizational adoption;
- provide rich technical feedback.

## Product expansion

1. Personal AI Coach
2. AI Task Receipts
3. Personal AI Work Graph
4. Project Memory
5. Team Intelligence
6. Enterprise Collaboration Governance
7. Cross-domain Human × AI Performance

## Defensibility

The moat is the longitudinal graph connecting user behavior, prompt patterns, agent execution, project context, outcomes, improvements, and reusable learnings.

Providers know what their models did. Pebl learns how the human and AI work together across providers.
