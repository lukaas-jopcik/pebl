# Product Principles

## 1. Never slow the user down

No mandatory task creation, completion buttons, modal interruptions, or repeated confirmations.

## 2. One precise insight beats ten generic tips

Pebl should remain silent when confidence is low.

## 3. Coach, do not judge

The product explains what happened, why it mattered, and what can improve. It never shames users or ranks employees by raw token usage.

## 4. Improvement must be measurable

Recommendations should connect to observable changes: fewer retries, better first-pass success, lower avoidable cost, stronger verification, or improved task completion.

## 5. Privacy by default

Code, prompts, and outputs stay local unless the user explicitly enables broader analysis.

## 6. Provider independence

Pebl must support built-in inference, BYOK, local models, OpenRouter, LiteLLM, and enterprise endpoints.

## 7. The project learns too

Useful learnings can become project memory, reusable prompts, or team rules.

## 8. Automation over administration

Tasks and sessions are inferred internally. Users interact with insights, not bookkeeping.
